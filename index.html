05 / MEET MICHAEL

COLONEL. ATTORNEY. AUTHOR.

Michael A. Graham spent nearly three decades in the U.S. Army Reserve, rising from an enlisted helicopter repair specialist to the rank of Colonel.

His military service includes combat deployments to Iraq and Afghanistan, senior legal leadership roles at Fort Bragg, the Pentagon, and within NATO-supported operations, and a Master of Strategic Studies from the U.S. Army War College.

In civilian practice, Michael is an AV Preeminent-rated commercial and M&A attorney, founding partner, and title company president based in South Carolina. He is also a private pilot, founder of the Liberty 250 civic initiative, and author of United Traits of America and the forthcoming Squared Away.

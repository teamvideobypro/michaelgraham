Meet Michael

Nearly 30 years in uniform. Two combat deployments. A career in law and business.

Michael Graham is a retired U.S. Army Reserve Colonel and an attorney and business leader based in Columbia, South Carolina. His assignments have included combat deployments to Iraq and Afghanistan, senior legal leadership roles, and earning a Master of Strategic Studies at the U.S. Army War College.

He holds a Law Degree and a Master’s in Public Administration, and is a founding partner of the commercial law firm D’Alberto, Graham & Grimsley, LLC.

He is the author of United Traits of America and the forthcoming Squared Away: A Soldier’s Guide to an Exceptional Life.
